White Label SEO Consultant

Deliver world-class SEO results under your own agency's brand. Partner with a veteran SEO professional to expand your capabilities, improve client retention, and grow your revenue without the overhead of hiring full-time staff.

What is White Label SEO?

White label SEO allows marketing agencies, web developers, design studios, and PR firms to sell premium search engine optimization services to their clients while outsourcing the actual strategy, analysis, and execution to a specialized consultant.

All deliverables, reports, and recommendations are custom-branded with your agency's logo and color palette, ensuring a seamless experience for your clients.

Why Partner with a White Label SEO Specialist?

  • Expertise on Demand: Instantly add deep technical, semantic, and off-site SEO expertise to your team without the lengthy recruitment and training processes.
  • Scalability: Accept new and larger clients with confidence, knowing you have the capacity and skill sets ready to support their needs.
  • Focus on Core Strengths: Free up your internal team to focus on sales, account management, design, or client relations while your SEO partner handles the research and optimization.
  • Client Retention: Elevate client satisfaction with detailed, high-impact strategies and reports that clearly demonstrate search visibility and organic traffic growth.

Core Services Available

  • In-Depth Technical Audits: Finding crawl errors, rendering issues, indexing blocks, site-speed bottlenecks, and architectural limitations.
  • Semantic & On-Page Strategy: Mapping keyword intent, building content hubs, optimizing existing landing pages, and guiding content creators with search-first guidelines.
  • Competitive & Gap Analysis: Studying competitor tactics, identify quick-win opportunities, and mapping out a long-term roadmap to outperform market leaders.
  • Bespoke Reporting: Customized dashboards or white-labeled static reports summarizing key performance indicators (KPIs) in clear, executive-friendly language.

How We Collaborate

We work in the way that best fits your agency's operational style. This typically follows one of two modes:

  • Behind-the-scenes (Silent Partner): All correspondence, files, and deliverables are submitted directly to your team. You manage all direct communication with the client.
  • Client-Facing expert: I join your client calls representing your agency as your internal "Lead SEO Specialist", matching your company's tone and values.
